# AB Nasdaq's VSL, element by element

Everything in `abnasdaqvsl.mp4` (6:13, 1920×1080, 30 fps), measured so Jean's VSL can copy it 1:1. How it was
measured: every cut detected (155 shots), every shot looked at, on-screen text read by OCR 5 times a second (1,864
frames), text sizes and colours measured on full-resolution frames, zooms tracked frame by frame, and the music
lined up sample by sample against "Me And The Devil" (instrumental). His full transcript is in
[AB-TRANSCRIPT.md](AB-TRANSCRIPT.md). Times are in AB's video.

## Music: "Me And The Devil" (instrumental)

It's the same song from the first frame to the last. There's no second track; the beat you hear under the proof
section is this song's own hi-hats and 808.

| | Measured |
| --- | --- |
| Level | Constant. The song sits at −29 dB of its own level the whole way (it never ducks under his voice and never rises in the gaps). His voice is at −24 LUFS, the song at −38.9 LUFS: **about 15 LU under his voice** |
| EQ | None. Every band from 40 Hz to 4 kHz is exactly −29.0 dB; 4–10 kHz is 1–3 dB higher only because of his own sound effects. The boom (808 sub-bass) is still in it at full strength |

## Side text (stacked lines beside his head)

- The hook and a few key lines move off the bottom and stack up in the empty side of the frame:
  "Every one / **OF THESE** / **VIDEOS**" (left) → "starts off / **THE SAME** / exact way" (right).
- Line 1 regular weight (about 100 px, Inter Display Medium), line 2 heavy caps (about 120 px, Inter Display Black,
  tight), line 3 either heavy caps in **blue #008CFC** or regular again.
- Bright shot: a **white fade** from that edge (strong at the edge, gone by the middle of the frame) with **black
  text**. Dark shot (his later, darker shots): a **dark fade** with **white text** ("The Syndicate runs / **for 90 days**").
- Each line builds on its word with the same blur-in reveal as the captions (letters come in left to right, blurred
  and spread, in about 0.25 s).
- A soft click on each line.

## Zoom

- One camera. Face shots alternate between **wide (1.0)** and **tight (about 1.2–1.25)**.
- The moves are quick: 0.3–0.8 s with a strong ease-out (most of the move in the first few frames, then it settles),
  e.g. 1.00 → 1.23 in 0.27 s then on to 1.27 over 0.3 s. Some changes are hard cuts between the two framings instead.
- Between moves the framing holds (2–6 s), sometimes with a slow drift of 2–3%.
- The moves land on sentence starts and key words, about every 3–5 s.

## Sound effects

His music is the plain song, so subtracting it from his mix leaves only his voice and the effects.

- A short, crisp **click/tick** on almost every piece of text that appears (each side-text line, each keynote word
  group, each counter). Bright (3–12 kHz), 30–80 ms, a little wider than his voice.
- **Whooshes** on bigger switches (into the money drop, the PIP card, the coin flying in, the slide transition).
- A low **impact** on "and the very next month" before "I blew $75,000".
- A **coin swish** as the Topstep coin flies in, and a **money flutter** when bills fly at the payout card.
- No ticking under the counters, no riser, no cash register.

## Pace

- 155 shots in 6:13. Outside the proof section a new shot or graphic about every 2.4 s; graphics often last only
  0.3–1.5 s.
- He never pauses: no dead air anywhere.
- His face is on screen for only about half of the video.
