## Before using any of these

- **36 of them are Discord posts** (dark theme, username, date, message, reactions). They read as real
  community proof, which is what Jean's script leans on ("ask them for yourself… the same Discord community").
  Use them as Discord cards: crop to the message and image, then zoom into the number.
- **Jean's surname.** He blacked it out on some certificates (**18.07.04**, **18.09.24**, `IMG_9100`) and in the
  emails in his screen recording, but it's readable on **18.12.03**, **18.11.45**, **18.11.36**, **18.11.24** and
  **18.09.41**, and as "Jean Yvan B." on **18.09.04**. If he wants it hidden, it gets blurred everywhere.
- **Students' names and avatars.** Several show full names (Lawrence Long, Shema Tevin, Imran Ismail, Mohamud M.,
  Mohammed Ameen). Check that each student is fine being in an ad, or blur names and avatars.
- **`IMG_9100.PNG` is actually an iPhone HEIC photo** with a `.PNG` name. Most viewers and editors won't open it
  as-is; the edit tools convert it.
- **Everything is a screenshot**: phone screenshots are vertical and Discord crops are odd sizes. On the 16:9 VSL
  they go in a frame on a branded background, never stretched full-screen.
