# AB Trades' thank-you video, element by element

Everything in `abnasdaq-thankyou.mp4` (2:41, 1280×720, 29.97 fps), measured so Jean's thank-you video can copy it
1:1. How it was measured: every cut detected (55 shots), every shot looked at, on-screen text read by OCR 5 times a
second, text sizes and colours measured on full-resolution frames, zooms tracked frame by frame, and the audio measured
(loudness, the music bed in the few gaps between words, and the sound under every transition). Full transcript:
[TRANSCRIPT.md](TRANSCRIPT.md). Example stills: [`frames/`](frames/). Times are in AB's video.

| Sound effects | A bright airy shimmer on every light-leak transition. Nothing else |
| Pace | 192 words per minute, only 7 pauses longer than 0.3 s (the pauses are cut out) |
| Loudness | −16.4 LUFS, loudness range 2.7 LU (very even), true peak −0.4 dBTP |

## Icons

- White **line icons** (location pin, hourglass, diamond with rising arrows, ringing phone) inside **white dashed circles**,
  the label under the icon ([08](frames/08-icons-in-dashed-circles.jpg)).
- They pop in one by one as he names each one ("time, location and financial freedom"), side by side, centred as a row.
- On text cards an icon sits above the first line (bulletproof vest, ringing phone).

## Transitions: light leaks

- **25 light-leak flashes**, on every switch between his face and a graphic, and on the zoom resets between face segments.
- A warm film burn (orange, yellow, white) sweeps over the outgoing shot, peaks for 4 frames (0.13 s) as a near-full-frame
  orange/yellow flash, and washes off the incoming shot. About 0.3 s in total
  ([03](frames/03-light-leak-on-face.jpg), [04](frames/04-light-leak-full.jpg), [05](frames/05-light-leak-over-card.jpg)).
- On his face it's a bright, warm overexposure; going to or from a green card it's a saturated orange/yellow burn.

## Zoom

- **Staircase punch-ins**: the frame holds, then steps in about 10–15% in about 0.3 s, holds again, steps again, every
  1.5–3 s, climbing from 1.0× to about 1.5× (e.g. 0:47.2–0:52: 1.0 → 1.07 → 1.16 → 1.19 → 1.27 → 1.5).
- At the top of a staircase it **resets to the wide shot on a light-leak flash** and starts again
  ([15](frames/15-zoom-wide.jpg) → [16](frames/16-zoom-tight.jpg)).
- The first shot opens tight (~1.6×) and pulls out to wide in about 0.6 s.
- The long face segments (18–25 s) run three or four staircases.

## Screens and proof

- **Discord carousel** (0:27): testimonial screenshots in a row on the green plate, sliding left continuously with
  motion blur, the captions over them.
- **Application card** (0:42): his application page as a white rounded card, blurred for privacy, scaling up from a
  tiny thumbnail to most of the frame in about 0.6 s.
- **Guru thumbnails** (1:16): four YouTube thumbnails, one after another, about a quarter-second each, each in its own
  frame, with a light leak over the first.
- **Testimonial wall** (2:11): his shot shrinks into a rounded card and slides up off the top while two rows of Discord
  testimonials scroll up underneath, then "Inside of" / "The Syndicate" builds over the wall.

## Music

- **Not "Me And The Devil"**: lined up against it, nothing matches (0.7–0.8 similarity, against 0.98+ for the real song
  in the main VSL). The track isn't identifiable from here.
- A **very soft, warm pad**: its energy sits between 80 and 500 Hz, with almost nothing above 2 kHz. Steady tones on G
  (G2, G3) with B and D, so a G chord. **No drums or beat** anywhere.
- About **18 dB under his voice**. With only 7 pauses longer than 0.3 s in the whole video, it's felt more than heard.
- Constant, never stops; it lifts about 3–4 dB in the low end for the last 40 s (from about 2:00, into the close).

## Sound effects

- A **bright, airy shimmer on every light-leak transition**: all of its energy is above 2 kHz (strongest at 9–18 kHz,
  up to 22 dB over the background), starting with the flash and lasting about 0.6 s. No low end, no boom.
- Nothing on the caption words, the icons or the cards (no clicks or pops measured).

## Sound mix

- −16.4 LUFS integrated, loudness range only 2.7 LU: his voice is heavily compressed and very even.
- True peak −0.4 dBTP.
