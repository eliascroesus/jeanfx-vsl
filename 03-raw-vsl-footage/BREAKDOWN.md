## Notes for the edit

- **Hook**: cut "to just start believing it". Flash the Discord payout posts on "screenshot after screenshot",
  then cut them dead on "but I'm going to be doing this one differently".
- **Proof walkthrough (1:31)**: it's the longest stretch and the hardest to follow, with a lot of "you guys can
  see" and a muddled Alpha Capital part (1:18–1:27: "a cap on an account… the old system… $1,000"). Tighten it to
  about a minute: keep one sentence per firm, speed up the scrolling like AB does, zoom into each number and land
  it as a big number. "My account is currently down 6%" (1:01) is honest and fits the "I'll show you what's true"
  angle; keeping or cutting it is a judgment call.
- **The jump cuts** between takes (list above) get hidden by a zoom change or a graphic, like AB's punch-ins.
- **"Look at my student [unclear]" (6:08)**: the student's name doesn't come through. Either Jean names him (Desmond
  fits: six payouts in a row, every post thanking Jean) or the line starts at "When he joined".
- **The 90-day part of the offer is confusing** (6:52–6:59): "This will go on for only about 90 days. If you've not
  made any profits from your account, go ahead and you'll be able to request a call once every two weeks." It
  reads as a different promise from the guarantee at 8:21. Cutting 6:52.4–6:59.9 (from "This will go on" up to "I'm
  willing to help you master this") keeps the offer clean, unless that line describes a real term of the program.
- **Length**: tightening the proof walkthrough and the filler brings it to about 7:00–7:30. AB's is 6:13.
- **Pictures for the parts that are only talking** (2:25–5:53 is three and a half minutes of talking head): keynote
  cards for the problem section, his own charts for the setup, and the story pictures listed as missing in the
  asset map.
